# Fabrica — 4-Week Social Media Content Calendar

**Status:** Draft for review · **Task:** M9
**Compliance:** Follows `brand/brand-guidelines.md` and `brand/positioning-statement.md`. No blacklist terms ("AI-powered", "revolutionary", "game-changing"). No invented metrics, testimonials, or logos. Claims are either demonstrable workflows or clearly labeled early-access statements.

---

## 1. Strategy at a glance

### Platforms and cadence

| Platform | Cadence | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Twitter/X** | 6 posts/week (5 weekday + 1 optional weekend) | Primary channel; threads and quick product beats |
| **LinkedIn** | 3 posts/week | Founder-led, longer-form repurposes of the strongest X pieces |
| **YouTube** | 1 video/week | Primary focus of video; screen-share demos and workflow walkthroughs |
| **Instagram** | 2 posts/week | Visual repurposes: quotes, before/after workflow cards, Reels cut from YouTube |

### Content pillars and target mix

| Pillar | Target | X posts / 4 wks | Anchor days |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Product Updates | 30% | 7 | Mon, Tue (thread), weekend |
| Builder Tips | 30% | 8 | Thu, Tue (thread), weekend |
| Founder Journey | 20% | 5 | Wed |
| Community | 20% | 5 | Fri, weekend |

LinkedIn, YouTube, and Instagram mirror these pillars at lower cadence; X is where the pillar math is held.

### Weekly rhythm

- **Mon — Product:** feature, improvement, or roadmap beat with one clear control point.
- **Tue — Thread:** deep-dive thread (product mechanism or builder workflow).
- **Wed — Founder story:** behind-the-scenes, lessons, learning in public.
- **Thu — Tip:** repeatable workflow or best practice founders can copy today.
- **Fri — Community:** discussion, showcase, or weekend-build prompt.
- **Sat/Sun (X only):** optional, casual — real setup, quiet observation, small win.

### Standing rules for every post

1. Lead with the outcome and the control point, then the mechanism. `Brief → Review → Approve → Ship`.
2. Every product claim maps to a demonstrable workflow or is labeled "early access."
3. Agents are tools with scope and a job — never mystical agency.
4. Orange = one point of action per visual. Forge Dark dominant. Steel Gray for metadata.
5. Keep lower-right corner of social images clear (platform UI overlays).
6. Never publish waitlist counts, time-saved, or quote-like numbers without team verification.

## 6. Platform production notes

### Twitter/X
- 6 posts/week; weekday rhythm is fixed, weekend is optional but valuable for reach.
- Threads: 6–8 tweets, hook stops the scroll, last tweet always a CTA.
- Visual: 1600×900 Forge Dark card + one orange focal point + clean lower-right.

### LinkedIn
- 3 posts/week, always repurposing the strongest X beat of that day.
- Founder voice, plain language, one control point named per post.
- No hashtag stuffing; 1–3 max, #TheNextAIExit reserved for launch week.

### YouTube
- 1 video/week (target: 4–8 min). Weekly topics set above.
- All demo videos screen-recorded from the real product; no fabricated UI.
- Titles match hooks for search alignment (e.g., "How approval gates keep you in control").

- 2 posts/week: 1 carousel (tip or before/after workflow) + 1 Reel cut from the YouTube video.
