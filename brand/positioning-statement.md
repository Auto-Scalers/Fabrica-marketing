# Fabrica Positioning Statement & Executive One-Pager

**Audience:** founders, builders, lean teams, and operators

## Positioning statement

For **founders and builders** who need to manage and ship fast with AI, **Fabrica is the agentic business platform** that orchestrates specialized agents without requiring technical fluency. Unlike **Cursor or GitHub Copilot**, which are designed first around helping developers write code, Fabrica gives you **command-center control over AI agents** across business and coding work: set the brief, see the work, approve consequential steps, and keep the budget in bounds.

**Short form:** Your business, automated. Your judgment, still in the loop.

## Executive one-pager

### Direct the crew. Keep the call.

#### Problem

Founders are expected to move product, research, marketing, and operations forward at once, but AI work is still scattered across chats, coding tools, and disconnected automations. A coding copilot can help write code; a browser agent can complete a task; neither gives a founder one clear place to direct several specialists, review their work, and decide what moves next. The result is more output to coordinate, with too little visibility into quality, cost, and risk.

#### Solution overview

Fabrica is a command center for directed agent work. Give a researcher, developer, marketer, or analyst a clear brief; run focused work in parallel; and see what is running, waiting, or ready for review. Use approval gates, checkpoints, and budget limits to keep consequential work within your rules, while isolated worktrees keep parallel code changes separated. Fabrica works with the models you choose: bring your own keys, pay providers directly for inference, and keep model spend visible.

#### Key differentiators

- **Business crews, not just coding assistance.** Direct specialized agents across research, product, marketing, analysis, and software work from one operating view.
- **Autonomy with accountable control.** Review plans, diffs, outputs, and high-risk actions at explicit approval gates instead of handing work to a black box.
- **Clear operational and cost boundaries.** Run parallel work in isolated spaces, set hard budget stops, and use your own model keys rather than an opaque credit pool.

#### How Fabrica differs

| If you are considering | What it is optimized for | Fabrica’s distinct role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cursor or GitHub Copilot | Faster developer workflows and code assistance | A founder-facing command center for coordinating business and coding crews with plain-language approvals. |
| Manus or Devin | Delegating an autonomous task | Visible, ongoing direction of a crew, with checkpoints, budget boundaries, and authority retained by the founder. |
| Replit, Bolt, Lovable, or v0 | Getting from idea to a web-app prototype | Controlled execution after the demo: research, build, review, ship, and maintain work across the business. |
| n8n, Zapier, or Make | Connecting known, repeatable workflows | Directing coding-capable agents through ambiguous work that needs a brief, judgment, and review. |

#### Social proof / traction

Fabrica is building its external proof base. No customer counts, performance claims, testimonials, or logos are approved for publication in this document. Until verified evidence is available, lead with demonstrable workflows: a clear brief, visible agent status, an approval gate, a budget boundary, and a reviewable output.

#### Call to action

**See the command center.** Put a focused business or coding workflow in motion, review the plan, and approve the work that matters.

## Usage notes

- Lead with the founder outcome and control point before naming the technical mechanism.
- Describe Fabrica as a command center for directed agent work; do not claim it replaces an IDE, a model, or a team.
- Do not publish pricing, customer metrics, time-saved claims, or testimonials until they are verified and approved.
- Pair the primary message with concrete workflow evidence whenever possible: **Brief the crew. Review the plan. Approve the run. Ship the output.**
