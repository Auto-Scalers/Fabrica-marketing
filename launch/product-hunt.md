## 3. First Comment

We built Fabrica because we kept watching founders become the bottleneck between a brief and a result — and it was happening to us too. Work lived across chats, spreadsheets, and tools. Output showed up without context. And the moment an AI result mattered, we couldn't point to where it came from or stop it before it ran. That gap — between "AI can do a task" and "I can run my business on it" — is the whole reason Fabrica exists.

Fabrica is different in three ways.

First, it treats agents as a crew with jobs, not a chat with a genie. Assign a researcher, a developer, a marketer, or an analyst a clear brief, and work runs in parallel — each in its own isolated workspace, so nothing collides. Second, control is a default, not a setting. Approval gates hold consequential work until you review it, and hard budget limits stop a run before it spends past your line. Third, you bring your own model keys. Fabrica is the factory; your providers are the fuel. No opaque token meters, no bundled credits.

We're not here to sell magic. Early access means integrations are still expanding, and we'll say so plainly as we go. If you're a technical founder, a solo builder, a lean team, or a non-technical entrepreneur who wants more capacity without handing over judgment — try Fabrica today and tell us where it breaks. We'll be in the thread all day.
