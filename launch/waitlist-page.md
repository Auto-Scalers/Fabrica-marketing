## Problem

Current AI coding tools are autocomplete with ambitions. They help you write a
file, but they still leave you as the bottleneck between every brief and every
result.

As a founder, you do not have time to babysit a chat window line by line. When
one task has to finish before the next starts, your capacity caps out at one
thing at a time — and the coordination eats your day.

---

## Solution Preview

### 1. Direct a crew, not a chat

Put several focused jobs in motion at once. Brief a specialist, give it scope,
and let the work run in parallel instead of queuing behind one conversation.

### 2. Approve before it ships

Set approval gates and budgets on consequential work. You decide what moves
automatically and what waits for your review — no delegation without control.

### 3. See the work, end to end

Tasks and outputs live in one visible surface, not scattered across chats and
spreadsheets. Know what is running, what is waiting, and what needs you — at a
glance.

## FAQ

**When will Fabrica launch?**

Early access opens in waves starting this quarter. Join the waitlist and you
will get a first-run invite before the general public. Integrations are still
expanding, and we will be honest about what is ready and what is not.

**How is this different from Cursor or Copilot?**

Cursor and Copilot are coding tools — they help you write code faster inside an
editor. Fabrica is a command center for directed agent work across coding and
business tasks. It coordinates multiple agents in parallel, keeps work visible,
and puts approval gates and budgets where you want control. You brief the crew;
the crew ships; you call the shots.

**Is Fabrica free?**

Early access is free while we validate the workflows. Pricing will follow what
founders actually run, not a guess. You will always know what you are paying
for before you pay.
