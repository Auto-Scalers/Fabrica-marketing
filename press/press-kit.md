## 1. Brand Assets

### 1.1 Logo

Use the approved Fabrica logo artwork only. Do not redraw, typeset a
replacement, trace it, apply effects, or alter its proportions. Until the final
asset package is published, use the Fabrica wordmark as plain text in working
documents.

**Clear space**

Keep clear space on every side equal to the height of the lowercase **a** in
the wordmark. No type, image edge, rule, or UI control may enter this area.

**Minimum size**

| Format | Wordmark | Symbol (when supplied) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Screen | 96 px wide | 24 px wide |
| Print | 25 mm wide | 7 mm wide |
| Favicon/app tile | Do not use the wordmark | 16 px minimum |

**Color variations**

| Background | Preferred logo | Use when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forge Dark or other dark surface | White / Steel Light | Default digital and product use |
| White or warm paper | Forge Dark | Print, documents, light pages |
| Molten Orange | Forge Dark | Short, high-energy promotional moments only |
| Photograph/texture | Solid logo on a clean overlay | The image has enough quiet contrast |

**Never:** place the logo on a busy image, use an unapproved gradient fill,
outline it, add a shadow, rotate it, stretch it, or render it below minimum
size. Use one logo color at a time.

**File formats:** SVG (primary), PNG at 240 px, 512 px, and 1024 px (wordmark,
dark-surface and light-surface variations; icon-only once the symbol ships).

### 1.2 Color palette

The palette begins in darkness and uses heat sparingly. Molten Orange is an
instruction, an emphasis, or a moment of progress — not wallpaper.

**Primary colors**

| Token | Hex | RGB | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Forge Dark** | `#16171A` | `22 23 26` | Primary canvas, wordmark on light, dark type |
| **Molten Orange** | `#E8590C` | `232 89 12` | Accent — primary actions, key emphasis, progress |
| **Steel Gray** | `#8F939E` | `143 147 158` | Secondary — labels, diagrams, secondary type on dark |

**Supporting palette**

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forge Surface | `#22242A` | Panels, cards, deep image overlays |
| Forge Edge | `#343842` | Dividers, restrained borders, inactive UI |
| Molten Light | `#FF8A3D` | Gradient start, hover/highlight, data emphasis |
| Molten Ember | `#B94308` | Active/pressed states, print emphasis |
| Steel Light | `#C2C6CC` | Subheads, borders on dark, quiet type |
| Steel Deep | `#5E636D` | Secondary UI and structure |
| Paper | `#FAF7F0` | Light canvas, documents, light-mode campaigns |
| Ink | `#292725` | Text on Paper |

**Rules:**

- Use Forge Dark as the dominant field on dark work (about 70–80% of the frame).
- Use Steel Gray to organize, label, and recede; it should not compete with a CTA.
- Use Molten Orange once per decision point. A page with five orange actions has no primary action.
- On dark surfaces, use white or Steel Light for long-form text — never Molten Orange.
- Meet WCAG AA contrast at minimum.

### 1.3 Typography

Fabrica typography is clear before it is distinctive. Use **Geist** and
**Geist Mono** whenever available.

| Job | Primary family | Weight/style | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display / headlines | Geist | 700–800 | Compact, declarative, sentence case by default |
| Subheads | Geist | 600–700 | Explain the promise; avoid stacked abstractions |
| Body | Geist | 400–500 | 16–18 px screen equivalent; generous line height |
| Navigation / meta | Geist | 500–600 | Small labels may be uppercase with tracking |
| Commands / technical proof | Geist Mono | 400–500 | Literal strings or system evidence only |

**Fallbacks:** `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` for Geist; `Consolas, monospace`
for Geist Mono. Do not substitute display serif faces, condensed novelty faces,
or generic "tech" fonts.

## 2. Company Boilerplate

### Short (one sentence)

**Fabrica is the command center for founders who want to direct specialized AI
agents across business and coding work — without losing visibility, approval,
or control.**

### Medium (two–three sentences)

**Fabrica is a command center for founders who need work moving in parallel,
without losing visibility, approval, or control over what their agents do.
Assign a clear brief, keep tasks visible, set approval gates and budgets, then
review real output before it moves forward. It is built for founders, solo
builders, and lean teams who want more capacity without handing over judgment.**

### Long (one paragraph)

**Fabrica gives founders a practical way to direct specialized agents across
business and coding work. Assign a clear brief, keep tasks visible, set approval
gates and budgets, then review real output before it moves forward. Researcher,
developer, marketer, and analyst agents work in parallel from plain-language
briefs; isolated worktrees keep parallel code changes separated; checkpoints
and hard budget stops keep consequential work inside the founder's rules. The
platform runs locally-first, works with the models customers choose
(bring-your-own keys, provider-direct inference billing), and never sells AI
credits, tokens, or agents. It is built for people who want more capacity
without becoming the coordination bottleneck — or spending their day stitching
tools, chats, and spreadsheets together.**

- **Auto-Scalers.** The operating group behind Fabrica's development.
- **Local-first.** The command center runs on the customer's machine; work and
  credentials stay local and customer-controlled, with client-side credential

  the customer chooses.

metrics, testimonials, customer logos, or benchmark results. Requests for proof
of any product claim should be directed to the contact above.

## 5. Screenshot Descriptions

Use these descriptions to capture or produce on-brand screenshots. All screens
should follow the brand's visual world: a digital foundry, not a literal one.
Start from Forge Dark, show honest product UI and visible task states, use one
Molten Orange focal point per image, and keep the lower-right corner clear for
platform overlays. Never include fabricated metrics, fake testimonials, or
unreadable microtext.

### 5.1 App UI — command center (hero shot)

**Purpose:** First impression; the "single place to direct the work" frame.

- Forge Dark background filling most of the frame. Left rail lists the crew by
  role — Researcher, Developer, Marketer, Analyst — each with a name and a live
  status chip (Running / Waiting / Ready for review).
- Central board organizes work as tasks and outputs, not chat bubbles: columns
  for Brief, In Progress, Ready for Review, Done. One task card is highlighted
  with a thin Molten Orange edge to mark the single decision point.
- Status metadata in Steel Gray: elapsed time, budget used, model in use. No
  fake numbers or avatars; a task like "Market research — APAC launch" reads as
  a real, generic brief.
- Top bar shows the Fabrica wordmark in Steel Light on Forge Dark.
- **Caption option:** "One command center for research, build, marketing, and
  analysis work — visible, reviewed, under control."

### 5.2 Agent orchestration — parallel crews in action

**Purpose:** Show several specialists moving at once without colliding.

- A routing view: the founder's objective at the left ("Launch pre-check"),
  branching into parallel agent lanes that diverge to separate worktrees.
- Each lane shows an agent role tag (Geist Mono for commands/technical labels),
  a task-state chip, and a short plain-language status line in Steel Light —
  e.g., "Researcher — interviewing 3 target segments," "Developer — isolating
  change in worktree `launch-fix`."
- Routing lines in Steel Gray with a single Molten Orange segment marking the
  path toward an approval point. Motion cue implied by direction, not
  decoration.
- **Caption option:** "Specialized agents run in parallel from clear briefs —
  each in its own isolated workspace."

### 5.3 Approval gate — the founder's call

**Purpose:** Prove human authority is a designed part of the workflow.

- A single task paused at its approval gate, visually isolated on a Forge
  Surface panel. The state reads: "Growth audit is ready for review. Approve
  the plan to start the run."
- On the panel: a reviewable plan (a diff or a short list of proposed steps in
  Geist Mono for the technical proof), budget shown, and one clear primary
  action — an orange **Approve** button beside a quiet Steel Gray **Edit brief
  & rerun** secondary action.
- One orange element per decision point; everything else recedes in steel tones.
- **Caption option:** "Review the plan before it runs. Approve, or edit the
  brief and rerun — the call stays with you."

### 5.4 Worktree view — parallel, isolated changes

**Purpose:** Show technical control without turning into a coding demo.

- A worktree board: several parallel changes listed as cards, each tied to a
  separate isolated workspace, with status chips (In progress / Awaiting review
  / Merged) in Steel Gray.
- One card expanded with a small readable diff preview in Geist Mono, and a
  single orange "Request review" action. Steel-light subheads, Forge Edge
  dividers between rows.
- No terminal wallpaper, no fake logs, no glowing effects.
- **Caption option:** "Parallel code changes stay separated in isolated
  worktrees, then move forward only when you review the output."
